Before the lesson

Share this site's GitHub Pages URL with students. Ask learners to use their school-approved browser and editor (e.g. Google Docs or Chromebook text editor). Each pair uses one Chromebook.

Suggested lesson: 5 min briefing, 25 min missions, 10 min report/verification, 5 min sharing (45 minutes total). The in-game timer defaults to 40 minutes after briefing.

Scoring: 10 + 10 + 15 + 20 + 15 = 70. Missions 1, 3 and 4 rely on students honestly checking their work and teacher inspection. Missions 2 and 5 automatically check multiple-choice responses.

Privacy: This is a static offline-capable-in-principle webpage (after loading), with no analytics, backend or class leaderboard. Team name, choices and progress are kept in this browser's local storage; other students cannot see them. Do not enter full student names. The teacher can inspect result screens or exported text files.

Made for Year 3 Digital Literacy · Be curious, kind and safe online 🌟
